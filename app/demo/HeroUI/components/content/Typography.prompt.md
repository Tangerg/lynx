Every piece of text in a HeroUI surface. Maps a visual `type` to the right semantic element.

```jsx
<Typography.Heading level={2}>Built for the intelligence age</Typography.Heading>
<Typography.Paragraph size="sm" color="muted">Secondary body and table cells.</Typography.Paragraph>
<Typography.Code>pnpm add @heroui/react</Typography.Code>
```

Scale: h1 36 · h2 30 · h3 24 · h4 20 · h5 18 · h6 16 (all 600 weight, tight tracking) · body 16/1.75 · body-sm 14/1.5 (the UI default) · body-xs 12/1.25.
